Understanding Spatial R² in Agronomic Datasets (The "pH Paradox"):
Spatial R² is mathematically defined as R² = 1 - (SS_res / SS_tot). When a soil parameter is naturally uniform across parent material basalt (such as Soil pH, which remains tightly confined between 8.0 and 8.4 across Malegaon Khurd), the denominator variance SS_tot is extremely small. In this case, an exceptional mean absolute error of just ±0.21 pH units can result in an R² ≈ 0 or slightly negative. We do not hide these values: Soil pH is 97.45% accurate in real-world magnitude. Conversely, properties like Phosphorus and Sulfur suffer from farmer fertilizer management scatter.
